Methodology & Clinical Fairness

  • Diverse Representation: Features both male and female patients to avoid single-demographic bias (addressing the historical limitation of female-only cohorts).
  • Male Patients Pregnancies: Biological males are fixed to 0 pregnancies, avoiding imputation artifacts and maintaining clinical physiological validity.
  • Leakage-free Pipeline: Impossible zeros in lab values (glucose, blood pressure, BMI) are treated as missing and imputed via KNN fitted strictly on training folds.
  • Class Imbalance: Balanced weights are applied across classes to prevent models from skewing toward majority non-diabetic cases.
  • 5-Fold Cross-Validation: Guarantees that performance is statistically reproducible and not the product of an advantageous random split.
